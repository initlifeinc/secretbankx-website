Which devices does it support?+

SecretBankX is designed for iPhone, iPad and native Mac. System features such as AutoFill depend on the platform, system settings and support from the destination app or website.

Does SecretBankX receive my vault?+

We do not operate a server that receives or stores vault contents. Optional sync sends encrypted files to storage you choose. Providers may still process IP addresses, connection and file metadata. Support emails you initiate send your selected message and attachments to the recipient.

How does password exposure checking work?+

Only when you explicitly start a check, the App connects directly to HIBP over HTTPS, sends the first five characters of a password’s SHA-1 hash and matches the response locally. It sends no plaintext password, full hash, username or website. The service can still observe your IP address, prefix and request time. It is not continuous background monitoring.

Does Family Premium share all my passwords?+

No. Apple Family Sharing shares purchase entitlement. Vault access still needs a separate invitation and role authorization. Personal Premium can also create shared vaults and invite members; invited members can participate for free within their permissions.

Can you recover a forgotten master password?+

We have no password-reset service that can decrypt your vault. Convenient unlock does not replace the master password needed for a manual full restoration. Protect your master password and keep verified, independent backups.

Are diagnostics uploaded automatically?+

No. Redacted diagnostics stay on device for up to 30 days and 50 MiB. Selected content leaves only when you export, share or send a support email. Review it first; never include passwords, recovery secrets or vault contents.
