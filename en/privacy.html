Core commitment: SecretBankX is local-first. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your vault contents. Optional encrypted sync uses storage you select, including iCloud Drive, Files-compatible folders or HTTPS WebDAV. The provider handles its own service under its terms.

1. Overview

SecretBankX is developed and operated by an individual developer under the InitLife brand, associated with the personal website initlife.com. InitLife is a brand and website name, not an incorporated company. In this Privacy Policy, “we” and “our” refer to that individual developer. This policy explains how information is handled when you use SecretBankX.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not receive your vault contents. Explicit AutoFill, export, encrypted sync, and optional network checks have the boundaries described below:

  • Plaintext passwords and vault contents are not uploaded for password checking; only a hash prefix is sent for an explicitly started exposure check
  • Your master password or any derived keys
  • The contents or structure of any Vault you create
  • Any biometric data that could identify you

2.2 Local Processing and User-Directed Transfers

Core vault processing takes place on your device. If you enable a transfer or network feature, information is sent to the destination you select as described below:

  • Device Biometrics: Face ID, Touch ID, or other system-supported biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple's system frameworks on-device. Only a success/failure result is returned; we cannot access raw biometric data.
  • Local Encrypted Storage: Encrypted Vault database files are stored in the App sandbox or App Group shared container so the main App and AutoFill Extension can access the same local data. We do not receive these files.
  • iCloud Drive Sync (if enabled): If you enable iCloud sync in the App, the App writes encrypted sync files, including recovery snapshots, incremental changes, and required attachments, to your own iCloud Drive container for syncing between your devices. A complete backup that you export manually is a separate operation. Files are encrypted before upload; Apple's privacy policy and iCloud terms govern iCloud storage and transfer.
  • AutoFill Extension: When system password AutoFill is enabled, the App registers matching website domains and credential identifiers on-device so iOS can show SecretBankX suggestions in Safari or other apps. Actual passwords are used for filling only after you select a credential and unlock.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi Transfer: When you explicitly start Wi-Fi Transfer, the App temporarily runs a local service on your device and generates a one-time access token for importing or exporting encrypted backups from a browser on the same network. This service does not send data to us.
  • Purchase Status: If the App offers paid features, purchases, subscriptions, refunds, and purchase restoration are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit. The App may read the local transaction state returned by Apple to unlock features; we do not receive your full payment information.
  • Files / HTTPS WebDAV (if enabled): The App writes encrypted sync objects and attachments to your chosen folder or WebDAV server. The storage service may receive connection metadata, file metadata and the server credentials needed for access. These are not your vault master password. Provider retention and deletion are governed by that provider.
  • Local Diagnostics: Redacted diagnostic records are stored locally, with a 30-day retention limit and a 50 MiB size limit. There is no automatic diagnostic upload. Only an export, system share or support email you explicitly initiate sends the selected file to its chosen recipient.

2.3 Information Collected via the App Store

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may process information related to downloads, purchases, subscriptions, refunds, crash reports, and install counts according to its privacy policy. We may review aggregated, anonymized data Apple provides to improve the App, but this cannot be linked to any individual.

2.4 When You Contact Us

If you contact us for support, we receive the sender details, message and any attachments you choose to send, including an optional diagnostic export. We use them to handle your request and do not sell them or use them for advertising. Review exports before sharing and do not include passwords, master passwords, recovery secrets or vault contents.

3. Data Storage & Security

All your credential data is protected by the following measures:

  • File-level encryption: Vault databases use SQLCipher with AES-256.
  • Key protection: Your master password protects an account data key and is not stored in plaintext. Optional convenient unlock may use a device-protected key in Keychain; it does not store your master password.
  • Memory protection: Unwrapped keys are held in memory for an authorized session and cleared when that session ends.
  • Sandbox and App Group isolation: The main App and AutoFill Extension share necessary data through a system-protected App Group container; other apps cannot access that container.
  • Encrypted sync and transfer: iCloud sync files and complete backups transferred over Wi-Fi remain encrypted before leaving the current App.

We do not operate a server that stores your vault contents. If you enable a storage provider, it stores encrypted files under its own security and availability controls.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data or use it for advertising tracking. User-directed transfers, optional password checks and support messages involve the recipients described in this policy; the absence of a developer-operated vault server does not mean these recipients receive no information.

Information you send us for support is used to answer your inquiry. Separately, optional functions you explicitly start may contact providers as described below.

5. Children's Privacy

SecretBankX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below.

6. Third-Party Services

SecretBankX does not integrate any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

The App uses Apple system services for App Store distribution and purchases, iCloud Drive sync, Keychain/biometrics, the AutoFill Extension, and local network features. Open-source libraries including SQLCipher, OpenSSL, nlohmann/json and zxcvbn-c run locally. Optional password exposure checks are off by default and start only when you tap Check. The App sends the first five hexadecimal characters of the password SHA-1 hash over HTTPS directly to HIBP Pwned Passwords, then matches the response locally. No plaintext password, full hash, username or website is sent. The service and its network providers can see the IP address, hash prefix and request time. Turning the feature off stops future requests but cannot retract requests already sent. Checks do not run while typing, saving, importing or syncing; results are kept only in session memory. HIBP states that operational and anti-abuse logs may include IP addresses and headers and be retained for up to 31 days. This is separate from the App keeping results only in memory. See https://haveibeenpwned.com/Privacy for the provider policy.

7. Your Rights

You control your local vaults and selected storage destinations. Support correspondence and provider records are handled separately:

  • Access & Export: Your data lives on your device and can be viewed at any time within the App.
  • Deletion: Removing a local vault removes its local copy and retains remote sync data. Remote deletion is a separate action with explicit confirmations and can affect other syncing devices. Independently exported backups, provider backups and other copies may require separate removal; uninstalling the App does not erase all remote copies.
  • Portability: The App supports data export, allowing you to migrate at any time.

If you wish to have any email correspondence you've sent us deleted, please contact us at initlifeltd@gmail.com and we will handle it within a reasonable timeframe.

Material changes will be communicated via in-app notification or App Store release notes.
